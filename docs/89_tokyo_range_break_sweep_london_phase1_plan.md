## Question and scope
Do London 08:00–09:00 breaks of a completed Tokyo 09:00–15:00 range lead to 09:00–11:00 continuation, or does a one sided sweep lead to reversal? This is market structure discovery, distinct from Tokyo direction research. Primary units are seven separate pairs, fixed order: USDJPY, EURJPY, GBPJPY, AUDJPY, AUDUSD, EURAUD, GBPAUD. Two pooled directional hypotheses per pair give exactly 14 primary tests. No trading PnL, SL, TP, spread, risk, lots, time optimization, filters, money or portfolio simulation. Volatility Phase 5 Dell Demo Forward, EA, SET, VPS and live are untouched.

## Daily eligibility, windows and exact boundaries
Candidate dates are Monday–Friday JST dates from 2015-01-01 through 2026-09-09 inclusive, including holidays as candidates. Each date uses its Tokyo range at [09:00,15:00) JST: exactly 360 distinct M1 bars required. Missing any bar -> `INSUFFICIENT_TOKYO_RANGE_DATA`, no event or outcome. TokyoHigh=max(M1 High), TokyoLow=min(M1 Low), TokyoRangePips=(High−Low)/pip. A nonpositive range is `DEGENERATE_TOKYO_RANGE`.
Map the same JST candidate date to the London local calendar date of its 15:00 JST instant. Event window [08:00,09:00) `Europe/London` requires exactly 60 distinct completed M1 bars; missing any -> `INSUFFICIENT_LONDON_EVENT_DATA`. If London 08:00 precedes Tokyo 15:00 UTC on an unusual date, exclude as `WINDOW_ORDER_INVALID`; never use an unfinished range. Require exact London 09:00 M1 Open for classification and exact London 11:00 M1 Open for outcome; no nearest bar fallback. Missing endpoints get `MISSING_LONDON_09_OPEN` or `MISSING_LONDON_11_OPEN`. Keep each exclusion reason and one primary exclusion status per pair/date in this priority order: source failure abort; Tokyo coverage; degenerate range; invalid order; London event coverage; 09 Open; 11 Open. Coverage and exclusions are counted, never imputed. London 09:00 bar High/Low cannot enter event detection. 11:00 Open cannot enter event classification.

High break is strictly M1 High > TokyoHigh; low break strictly M1 Low < TokyoLow. Equal touch is not a break. At London 09:00, Open > TokyoHigh means above, Open < TokyoLow means below, and equality at either boundary is inside. Assign exactly one: BOTH_SIDES if both breaks; NO_BREAK if neither; UP_BREAKOUT if high only and Open > TokyoHigh; UP_SWEEP if high only and Open <= TokyoHigh; DOWN_BREAKOUT if low only and Open < TokyoLow; DOWN_SWEEP if low only and Open >= TokyoLow. BOTH_SIDES and NO_BREAK are diagnostic only. A high-only event whose 09 Open has crossed below TokyoLow is still UP_SWEEP under this fixed rule (analogous low-only crossing above TokyoHigh is DOWN_SWEEP). Outcome for every eligible class is (London 11:00 Open − London 09:00 Open)/pip, with .01 for JPY quote pairs and .0001 otherwise. No rounding before statistics.

## Hypotheses and periods
BREAKOUT_CONTINUATION pools UP_BREAKOUT with +outcome and DOWN_BREAKOUT with −outcome. SWEEP_REVERSAL pools UP_SWEEP with −outcome and DOWN_SWEEP with +outcome. Pooling is by event day; no direction selection. Primary metric is mean aligned pips. For every pair, hypothesis and period report events, observed Monday based calendar weeks, mean, median, sample SD (ddof=1), total aligned pips and positive/negative/zero rates (all events in denominator).
Historical 2015-01-01–2021-12-31; Recent A 2022-01-01–2023-12-31; Recent B 2024-01-01–2025-12-31; 2026 Monitor 2026-01-01–2026-09-09; Recent Combined 2022-01-01–2026-09-09; ALL 2015-01-01–2026-09-09. Bounds inclusive by JST candidate date. 2022–2026 is not pristine unseen holdout.
Formal sample for ALL, Historical, Recent Combined: >=40 events and >=20 observed calendar weeks. Each of Recent A, Recent B, 2026 Monitor: >=20 events and >=10 observed weeks. Below threshold is `INSUFFICIENT_SAMPLE`; its mean cannot satisfy any support gate. Descriptive numbers may still appear.

## Bootstrap, p and multiplicity
For each pair × hypothesis × period, cluster observations by Monday based JST calendar week. The population of clusters is every calendar week intersecting the fixed period, including weeks with no events. With NumPy `default_rng(20260913)`, reset the seed per period and reuse the same 5,000 sampled week index vectors across all pair/hypothesis cells in that period; sample K weeks with replacement per replicate and retain all events of each selected week. A replicate with zero events is invalid. Require >=4,750 valid replicates for inferential status; otherwise `BOOTSTRAP_INSUFFICIENT`. Percentile 95% CI is the linear interpolated 2.5/97.5 quantile of valid replicate means. Two sided centered bootstrap p for H0 mean=0 is `(1 + count(|mean* − observed_mean| >= |observed_mean|)) / (1 + valid_replicates)`. The p and percentile CI need not be dual. Compute descriptive period CI when possible; only the 14 ALL pair × hypothesis p values enter one Holm family. Missing/invalid raw p is treated as 1 for Holm ordering while recorded as missing. Stable tie order is the fixed pair order, then BREAKOUT_CONTINUATION before SWEEP_REVERSAL. Holm adjusted p is cumulative maximum of (14−rank+1)×sorted p, capped at 1. Alpha is 0.05; adjusted p < 0.05 is required.
